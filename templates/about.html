About SafeTransit-AI

A computer-vision research platform designed to automate road incident detection, quantify scene severity, and expedite field alerting.

System Architecture & Core Capabilities

Architecture Brief

Urban traffic infrastructure worldwide suffers from delayed emergency response times when vehicular collisions occur in blind spots or unattended CCTV segments. Traditional surveillance requires human operators to monitor hundreds of concurrent feeds. SafeTransit-AI automates this workflow by continuously analyzing video streams with computer-vision models to identify collision events the moment impact occurs.

1. Object & Collision Detection
Custom-trained YOLOv8 weights (best.onnx) executed through native C++ OpenCV DNN with letterbox scaling (640x640), Non-Maximum Suppression, and dual-class mapping for car and accident.
2. Situational Severity Estimation
RandomForest classifier (severity_model.pkl) evaluating scene impact metrics across a 4-feature schema (detection count, mean confidence, bounding box pixel area, and class index) into fatal, major, or minor visual ratings.
3. Telemetry & Field Alerts
Verified Telegram Bot integration (@SafeTransitAccidentBot) featuring sliding cooldown suppression (60s), photographic evidence snapshots, and formatted incident timestamps.
